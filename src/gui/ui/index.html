Mode
Turbo fixes flags Roblox reverts mid-game almost instantly (no in-game flicker), using a tight background loop for a small extra CPU cost. Watchdog re-checks every few seconds — lighter on the system.
FPS Unlocker ?
On (recommended): FPS uncapped via Roblox's settings file; FPS-cap flags are disabled (shown with a yellow ? in the editor). Off: the lock is released so your own FPS flags take effect.
Automatic Launch
Launch Roblox through FFM ?
Off (default): FFM opens only when you open it; clicking Play launches Roblox directly. On: clicking Play launches through FFM so your flags apply automatically.
